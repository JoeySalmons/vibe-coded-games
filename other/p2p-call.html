P2P Call

SERVERLESS

Encrypted peer-to-peer video. No account, no server holding your stream — just a shared passphrase and a public notice board for the handshake.

How it connects: your passphrase + the date are run through PBKDF2→HKDF to derive a daily ntfy.sh topic and an AES-GCM key. The first person in becomes the host; the second joins automatically. Offers expire after 90 min and the room rotates every UTC day.

One catch: peers behind symmetric NAT (some mobile/corporate networks) may fail to connect — there's no TURN relay. Try a different network if it hangs on "connecting".
